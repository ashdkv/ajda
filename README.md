## What is Ajda

Ajda is an independent navigation and city guide for Serbia — think of it as a
2GIS-style service for the region. It combines a vector map, place search,
public transport timetables and navigation on foot, by bike, scooter, car and
transit.

- **Public transport everywhere in Serbia** — city buses and trams (Novi Sad,
  Belgrade, Niš, Kragujevac, …), intercity buses (polazak.rs, MAS Novi Sad and
  carrier timetables) and Srbijavoz trains, with live service notices.
- **Door-to-door routes** — walking, bike, e-scooter, car and public transport;
  turn-by-turn voice navigation, "I'm already on the bus" mode, rerouting.
- **Places** — shops, pharmacies, ATMs, cafes, sights; opening hours, photos,
  ratings and reviews, "what's here" by tapping the map.
- **Guide for newcomers** — practical guides for visitors and people moving to
  Serbia (tickets, SIM cards, airport transfers, residence permits).
- **Offline maps** for major cities and regions (Android).
- **Languages:** Serbian (Latin/Cyrillic), Russian, English.

## Open data

We publish a validated open **GTFS feed** for Novi Sad public transport
(0 errors in MobilityData's validator): <https://feeds.ajda.rs>.

Ajda is built on open data and gives credit where it is due:
[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) (ODbL),
[Overture Maps](https://overturemaps.org), Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons,
[Protomaps](https://protomaps.com), data.gov.rs (SODL) and carriers' public
timetables.
